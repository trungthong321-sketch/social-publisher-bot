Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Social Publisher Bot helps users manage and publish their own content to supported social media platforms.

1. Information We Process

The service may process account authorization information, access tokens, uploaded media, captions, and publishing instructions provided by the user.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the requested publishing functionality, authenticate connected accounts, and complete user-requested actions.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to supported social media platforms only when necessary to perform actions requested by the user.

4. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and account authorization information.

5. User Control

Users may disconnect supported accounts or revoke platform authorization through the relevant social media platform.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service changes or when required by applicable platform policies.
